Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

Creator Stack Lab respects user privacy. We collect minimal personal information:

  • Voluntary Information: Name and email address when you voluntarily submit a message through our Contact form.
  • Anonymous Technical Data: Standard server log details such as IP address (anonymized), browser type, referring URLs, and pages visited to evaluate aggregate traffic patterns.

2. Cookies & Third-Party Partner Tracking

When you click on an outbound partner link marked with rel="sponsored", the partner platform (e.g. PartnerStack, FirstPromoter, or Impact) may store a cookie on your browser to track referrals and ensure commission attribution. These cookies do not store personally identifiable data and expire within 30 to 60 days depending on the vendor's policy.

3. Data Protection & Your Rights

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. If you wish to review or request the deletion of any correspondence sent to us, please contact us at our contact page.
