FTC Affiliate Disclosure & Transparency Policy

Compliance with 16 CFR Part 255

Our Commitment to Reader Trust

The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) requires digital publishers to disclose any material connection between a reviewer and a product merchant. At Creator Stack Lab, we embrace full transparency as a cornerstone of our work.

How We Earn Compensation

Some of the links on this website are affiliate links. This means that if you click on a link and subsequently sign up for a trial, register for an account, or purchase a paid subscription, Creator Stack Lab may earn an affiliate commission from the software provider.

This commission is paid by the software vendor and incurs zero additional cost to you as a user. In some cases, our partnerships may grant you access to extended free trials, discounted introductory pricing, or exclusive creator templates.

Editorial Independence Guarantee

Our commercial relationships do not determine our editorial judgments. Specifically:

  • Vendors cannot pay for higher rankings, favorable placement, or the removal of documented trade-offs.
  • We frequently review and recommend tools that offer zero affiliate compensation if we determine they represent the best solution for creators.
  • Our testing evaluations are based on verified technical documentation, public pricing terms, and functional capabilities.

Link Attributes & Search Standards

In accordance with search engine webmaster guidelines and consumer protection laws, all outbound partner links on our website are explicitly tagged with rel="sponsored" or rel="nofollow" attributes.
